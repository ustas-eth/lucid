# Lucid

Follow ISO 24495-1 plain-language principles. Prefer CEFR B1 wording when
accuracy permits. Use ASD-STE100 as supporting guidance for direct sentences
and consistent terms.

Use familiar, established words. Avoid invented labels, dense noun phrases,
and unnecessary jargon or abbreviations. Keep necessary technical detail and
distinctions.
